3. Scope of Work

We will perform only the services described in your accepted estimate or service agreement. Depending on the services selected, the scope may include:

  • Lawn maintenance: mowing, string trimming, edging of walks, drives and beds, and blowing of clippings from hard surfaces.
  • Shrub, hedge and topiary work: hand and power trimming, shaping and renewal pruning of shrubs and hedges.
  • Tree trimming: pruning of ornamental and residential trees reachable safely from the ground or a ladder, including structural, deadwood and clearance pruning. We do not perform hazardous tree removals, crane work, or work near energized power lines.
  • Cleanup: leaf removal, debris removal, bed edging, cutting back perennials, and removal or on-site composting of yard waste generated by our work.
No-pesticide policy. We do not apply synthetic pesticides, herbicides, or fertilizers. We rely on mechanical removal, proper pruning, and soil health. We will not apply such products even at a Client's request. Results of chemical-free care, especially weed suppression, develop over time and are not identical to results achieved with chemical programs.

Changes to scope. Any change, addition or deletion to the scope of work must be agreed in a written addendum (email is acceptable) that describes the change and any price adjustment, and is acknowledged by both parties. Our crew members are not authorized to agree to scope changes verbally. Work performed at your written request beyond the original scope will be billed at our then-current rates.

Exclusions. Unless expressly stated in your estimate, services do not include irrigation repair, hardscape construction, grading, drainage work, pest or disease treatment, snow removal, or the purchase and installation of plants and materials.

Recurring service pricing may be adjusted at the start of a new season or with at least thirty (30) days' written notice.

Ending recurring service. Either party may end a recurring service arrangement with forty-eight (48) hours' written notice. You remain responsible for services already performed.

Weather. Rain, saturated ground, lightning, extreme heat, ice, or other unsafe conditions may require us to reschedule. Weather delays are not considered cancellations by the Company and do not entitle the Client to a refund or credit. We will reschedule affected work as soon as practical, which may shift your regular service day.

Company cancellations. If we must cancel for reasons other than weather, we will reschedule promptly. If we cannot, you will not be charged for the missed visit.

7. Property Access and Client Responsibilities

You agree to:

  • Provide safe, clear access to the work area on scheduled service days, including gate codes or keys where needed.
  • Provide access to an outdoor water supply where required for the services, at no charge to us.
  • Secure all pets indoors or away from the work area during service. We may decline to perform work, and bill for the visit, if pets are loose in the work area.
  • Remove toys, hoses, furniture, pet waste and other objects from lawns and beds before service. We are not responsible for damage to items left in the work area.
  • Inform us of hidden hazards, including holes, septic components, wells, low wires, wasp nests, aggressive animals, and any underground features.
  • Inform us of HOA rules, easements or restrictions that apply to the Property.
