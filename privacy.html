1. Who We Are and Scope

This Privacy Policy explains how Topiary & Turf Estate Care LLC ("we," "us," "our") collects, uses, shares and protects personal information when you visit topiaryestatecareky.com (the "Site"), request an estimate, or use our landscaping services. By using the Site or our services, you acknowledge the practices described here.

2. Information We Collect

Information you provide

  • Contact and estimate forms: your name, email address, phone number, property address, the service you're interested in, and any details you include in your message.
  • Service records: property notes such as gate codes, pet information, access instructions, service history and photographs of completed work.
  • Communications: emails, texts, calls and messages you send us.

Payment information

Payments are handled by a third-party payment processor. Card and bank details are entered directly with that processor and are subject to its privacy policy and security standards. We do not receive or store your full card number. We may receive limited information such as your name, the last four digits of a card, payment amount and transaction status.

Information collected automatically

When you visit the Site, we and our analytics provider may automatically collect technical information such as your IP address, browser type, device type, referring page, pages viewed, and the date and time of your visit, using cookies and similar technologies (see Section 5).

3. How We Use Your Information

We use personal information to:

  • Respond to inquiries and provide quotes and estimates;
  • Schedule services, coordinate crew visits and communicate about weather delays;
  • Perform the services you request and keep accurate property records;
  • Send invoices, process payments and maintain financial records;
  • Send occasional seasonal reminders (for example, spring cleanup or fall leaf removal scheduling). You may opt out at any time;
  • Improve the Site and our services, and understand how visitors use the Site;
  • Comply with legal, tax and accounting obligations and protect our legal rights.
Opting out of reminders. Every reminder email includes an unsubscribe option, or you can email info@topiaryestatecareky.com with "Unsubscribe." Opting out of reminders does not affect messages necessary to provide services you've booked, such as scheduling confirmations and invoices.

4. How We Share Information

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising. We share information only as follows:

  • Payment processors, to process payments and refunds;
  • Scheduling and invoicing software providers that store client and job records on our behalf;
  • Service providers that help operate the Site (such as hosting, form delivery and Google Analytics), which may process data only on our instructions;
  • Legal requirements: when required by law, subpoena or court order, or to protect the rights, property or safety of our clients, our company or others;
  • Business transfers: in connection with a merger, sale or transfer of our business, subject to this Policy.

5. Cookies and Google Analytics

The Site uses Google Analytics, a web analytics service provided by Google LLC, to help us understand how visitors find and use the Site so we can improve it. Google Analytics uses cookies to collect information such as pages visited, time on page, and general location derived from IP address. Google's use of this information is governed by Google's own privacy policy. You can learn more about how Google uses data at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Your choices. You can disable or delete cookies in your browser settings. Most browsers let you block all cookies, block third-party cookies, or alert you when a cookie is set. You can also install the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on. Disabling cookies will not stop you from using the Site or our contact form.

The Site may also load fonts, images and maps from third parties (for example, Google Fonts, Google Maps and image hosting services). These providers may receive your IP address as part of delivering that content.

6. Data Retention

We keep client, invoice and payment records for seven (7) years for tax and accounting purposes. Estimate requests that do not result in service are generally deleted within two (2) years. Analytics data is retained according to our Google Analytics settings. When information is no longer needed, we delete it or de-identify it.

7. Data Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical and physical safeguards to protect personal information, including access controls on our business accounts and reputable, secure service providers. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, however, and we cannot guarantee absolute security. If we learn of a security breach affecting your personal information, we will notify you as required by Kentucky law.

8. Your Choices and Rights

You may contact us at any time to:

  • Request access to the personal information we hold about you;
  • Correct inaccurate information;
  • Request deletion of your information, subject to our legal record-keeping obligations (such as the seven-year tax retention period);
  • Opt out of seasonal reminders and marketing communications.

Depending on the size and nature of our business, certain state consumer privacy laws, including the Kentucky Consumer Data Protection Act, may or may not apply to us. Regardless, we honor reasonable requests from all clients. We will respond within forty-five (45) days and may need to verify your identity before acting on a request. We will not discriminate against you for exercising these choices.

9. Children's Privacy

The Site and our services are intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us and we will delete it.

10. Third-Party Links

The Site links to third-party websites, such as our partners and Google Maps. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those sites and encourage you to review their policies.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The updated version will be posted on this page with a revised "Last updated" date. Material changes will be highlighted on the Site or communicated to active clients by email.
