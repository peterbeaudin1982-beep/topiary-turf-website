Services

Landscaping services for Lexington estates

From weekly estate lawn care in Lexington to precise shrub trimming and ornamental tree work, every service is done by hand-trained crews using mechanical methods only. Prices below are starting points based on typical Lexington, KY properties in 2026.

Mowing & Edging

Estate Lawn Maintenance

Starting at $60–$95per visit · standard residential lots

Our weekly and bi-weekly lawn care keeps Lexington lawns dense, even and green the natural way. We mow at the right height for our cool-season tall fescue and bluegrass, cutting with freshly sharpened blades so every blade of grass heals cleanly.

  • Mowing at a height suited to the season, typically 3–4 inches
  • Sharp, string-trimmed and hard-edged borders along walks, drives and beds
  • Blowing of hard surfaces and clipping cleanup
  • Rotating mow patterns to prevent ruts and soil compaction
  • Seasonal notes on thin spots, drainage or turf stress

Premium estate pricing for larger acreage, fencing lines and formal grounds is quoted after a site visit.

Topiary

Shrub & Hedge Sculpting

Starting at $150–$400per project · based on count and complexity

This is where our name comes from. We hand-shape boxwood, yew, holly and privet into clean geometric hedges, soft cloud forms, or classic balls and cones. Each cut is chosen for the plant's long-term structure and good airflow, not just a quick flat-top.

  • Formal hedge lines kept level and plumb
  • Boxwood, yew and holly shaping and maintenance
  • Topiary forms: spheres, cones, tiers and cloud pruning
  • Renewal pruning for overgrown shrubs, staged across seasons
  • All clippings hauled away or composted

Large hedge walls and multi-day topiary restoration are quoted individually.

Ornamental / Residential

Tree Trimming & Canopy Care

Starting at $250–$900per tree · depending on size and access

Lexington's dogwoods, redbuds, Japanese maples and young shade trees reward patient, proper pruning. We make clean cuts at the branch collar following accepted arboricultural practice to improve structure, light and clearance without topping.

  • Structural and formative pruning of young trees
  • Deadwood, crossing and rubbing limb removal
  • Crown raising for clearance over lawns, walks and drives
  • Ornamental pruning for form and flowering
  • Seasonal timing chosen for each species

Please note: we do not perform hazardous tree removals. For storm-damaged or high-risk trees we gladly refer you to a certified arborist.

Leaf Removal & Bed Refresh

Seasonal Cleanups

Starting at $300–$800per cleanup · based on property size

Kentucky's autumns are glorious, and messy. Our spring and fall cleanups reset the whole property: leaves gone, beds defined, perennials tidied, and fresh natural mulch laid down to protect the soil through the season ahead.

  • Leaf removal from lawns, beds and hardscape
  • Hand-cut bed edges and debris clearing
  • Cutting back perennials and ornamental grasses
  • Natural, undyed mulch installation (materials quoted separately)
  • Mulching-mow of light leaf cover to feed the soil

Final pricing depends on leaf volume, bed footage and haul-away needs.

Our promise

The No-Pesticide Guarantee

"We do not apply synthetic pesticides, herbicides, or fertilizers. We rely on mechanical removal, proper pruning, and soil health."

Mechanical removal

Weeds are pulled, dug or cut, never sprayed. Beds are kept clean by hand and protected with natural mulch.

Proper pruning

Correct cuts and good airflow prevent most disease and pest pressure before it starts.

Soil health

Mulched clippings, compost topdressing, aeration and overseeding feed the soil life that feeds your lawn.

Questions

Frequently asked

How do you control weeds without herbicides?

Through mechanical removal, correct mowing height, mulching, overseeding and building soil health so dense turf crowds weeds out. A chemical-free lawn takes a season or two to settle in, and it gets stronger every year after.

Will my lawn look as good as a sprayed lawn?

It will look healthy, even and well kept. You may see the occasional clover, which feeds pollinators and fixes nitrogen in the soil. Most clients come to love it.

Do you remove hazardous trees?

No. We prune ornamental and residential trees but do not perform hazardous removals. We're happy to refer you to a certified arborist.

Which areas do you serve?

Lexington, Fayette County, and surrounding estates. If you're just beyond, ask anyway.

Are you insured?

Yes, we're fully insured. A certificate of insurance is available on request.
