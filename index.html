The kind of landscaping Lexington doesn't have to think about

Topiary & Turf Estate Care looks after lawns, hedges and ornamental trees across Lexington and Fayette County. The goal is a property that looks effortless every day of the season.

We work the old-fashioned way. Sharp blades keep turf healthy. Hand pruning shapes hedges, careful mulching keeps beds tidy, and soil that's treated well needs no chemistry. Your children, pets and pollinators can be on the lawn the moment we leave.
