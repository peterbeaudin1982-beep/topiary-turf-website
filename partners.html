Organizations we work with and rely on

Good landscaping in Lexington depends on good people nearby: growers who raise healthy native plants, dealers who keep equipment sharp, and the agencies and educators who keep our work safe, licensed and grounded in science.

Kentucky's statewide trade association for nursery growers, landscape professionals and garden centers. KNLA's education programs and events keep us current on best practices, plant health and professional standards across the Commonwealth.

A licensed Kentucky nursery growing local-genotype native wildflowers, grasses, shrubs and trees. When a bed refresh calls for new plantings, we look first to regionally native stock that supports pollinators and thrives without chemical inputs.

Safety

Kentucky 811

Kentucky's free "call before you dig" service. Before any work that disturbs soil, such as edging new beds or installing plantings, we request utility locates to protect underground lines on your property.
