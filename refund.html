1. Overview

We want every client to love their grounds. This Refund Policy explains how estimates, deposits, corrections and refunds work for services provided by Topiary & Turf Estate Care LLC. It forms part of our Terms & Conditions. Our first goal is always to make the work right.

2. Estimates

Written estimates are valid for fourteen (14) days from the date issued. After 14 days, pricing and availability may change and we may need to re-inspect the Property before confirming a price. Estimates are free and create no obligation until accepted.

3. Deposits

Deposits for larger projects are refundable only if you cancel before materials are ordered or before a crew is dispatched to your Property.

  • Cancelled before materials are ordered and before crew dispatch: full refund of the deposit.
  • Cancelled after materials are ordered or after crew dispatch: the deposit is non-refundable. Where possible, we will apply any portion not used for materials, restocking fees or labor already incurred as a credit toward future services.

Cancellations must be made in writing (email is fine) to info@topiaryestatecareky.com. The date and time we receive your message determines eligibility.

4. Recurring Services

24-hour re-service promise. If you are unhappy with a mow, trim or edge, notify us within twenty-four (24) hours of the visit and we will return to fix it at no charge.

Please include a brief description and, if possible, a photo so our crew knows exactly what to correct. Because recurring services are performed and consumed on the day of the visit, refunds are not issued for completed recurring services. Our remedy is to return and correct the work. Concerns reported after 24 hours will be addressed at the next scheduled visit.

Visits missed because of weather are rescheduled, not refunded, as described in our Terms & Conditions.

5. One-Time Projects

For one-time projects such as large seasonal cleanups, hedge restorations or tree pruning, refunds are not issued after work is completed.

If you have a concern about a completed project, contact us within seventy-two (72) hours. We will inspect the Property with you, in person or by photos, and where the work did not meet the agreed scope or our workmanship standards, we will offer a correction by returning to complete or redo the affected portion at no additional charge.

6. Plant Material

Because living plants are affected by weather, watering, soil, pests and care after installation, no refunds are given on plants installed (living material). We source healthy stock from reputable Kentucky nurseries and will gladly advise on watering and aftercare. If a supplier offers its own plant guarantee, we will pass that guarantee through to you where possible.

7. Eligible Refunds

Refunds may be issued in limited circumstances, for example:

  • A deposit cancelled within the eligible window described in Section 3;
  • Duplicate or erroneous charges;
  • Prepaid services we are unable to perform or reschedule for reasons within our control.

8. Refund Processing

Approved refunds are issued to the original payment method within ten (10) business days of approval. Depending on your bank or card issuer, it may take additional time for the refund to appear on your statement. Payments made by check or cash will be refunded by check mailed to your billing address. Payment processor fees are refunded where the processor returns them to us.

9. Disputes and Chargebacks

Please contact us before disputing a charge with your bank. We respond quickly and would much rather fix the problem directly. Initiating a chargeback for services that were performed may result in suspension of services until the matter is resolved.
